# Business Context — Fictional Pilot Shop

**Status:** fictional learning scenario. Details marked undecided are not facts about a real organisation.

## Pilot profile

| Item | Working choice | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| Organisation | Small retail shop | A small business that sells goods to customers |
| Product category | Stationery and office supplies | Pens, notebooks, paper, folders and similar goods |
| Product condition | Standard, non-perishable goods | Avoid expiry-date complexity in the first workflow |
| Initial problem | Reliable stock tracking | Explain what entered stock, what did not, and why |
| First process | Supplier-goods receiving | Check a delivery and record accepted goods |
| Possible users | Manager and accountant | Their precise permissions are not yet specified |
| Data | Fictional examples only | Do not use real company or private data |

## Problem statement

The shop needs a reliable way to record stock entries, identify differences between ordered and received goods, and trace the information supporting each stock movement.

## Initial objective

Help an authorised person record a delivery, identify the actual goods and their condition, record accepted quantities, update usable stock once, and revisit the history to understand why the quantity changed.

## Possible role responsibilities

- **Manager (*gérant/responsable*):** may oversee stock, review discrepancies and authorise decisions or amendments, depending on the procedure adopted for the fictional shop.
- **Accountant (*comptable*):** may compare orders, receipts and later supplier invoices, and prepare information for the accounting work covered in class.

These are proposed responsibilities for the scenario, not universal job descriptions. One person may perform multiple roles in a small business. Invoice processing and formal accounting automation are not part of the first implementation.

## Not decided and not needed yet

The fictional shop's name, employee count, current paper/spreadsheet process, full document set, detailed permission structure, reports and invoice workflow do not need to be invented to start the first console workflow. Define them only when a concrete scenario requires them.
